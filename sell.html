THE SMART SELLER'S HANDBOOK

Sell Smarter. Get More From Your Car.

A successful car sale starts with the right preparation. Explore practical tips to price your vehicle, attract genuine buyers, evaluate offers, and complete your sale confidently.

01 — 05

Your Roadmap to a Better Sale

From setting a competitive asking price to transferring ownership, every step matters. Use this guide to prepare your listing and make informed decisions throughout the selling process.

✦

Seller tip Be transparent about your car's condition, service history, and known issues. Honest listings help set the right expectations.

View Seller Checklist ↗
01
PRICING STRATEGY

Price Your Car Competitively

Compare similar cars by model, variant, year, mileage, location, and condition. Set a realistic asking price based on current listings and available valuation information.

  • Research comparable vehicles.
  • Account for mileage and maintenance.
  • Allow reasonable room for negotiation.
Estimate Your Car's Value →
02
PHOTOGRAPHY

Make Your Car Stand Out in Photos

Clean your vehicle before taking photographs. Use natural daylight, a tidy background, and clear angles to help buyers understand what you are selling.

  • Capture front, rear, side, and interior views.
  • Show the dashboard and odometer clearly.
  • Photograph visible damage honestly.
Create Your Listing →
03
DOCUMENT PREPARATION

Keep Your Documents Ready

Prepare the original registration certificate, valid insurance details, PUC certificate where applicable, service records, and other paperwork relevant to your vehicle and transaction.

  • Check the RC and registered owner details.
  • Review loan or hypothecation status, if applicable.
  • Confirm the required transfer forms and documents.
Review Seller Checklist →
04
OFFER EVALUATION

Evaluate Buyer Offers Carefully

Consider more than the quoted amount. Compare buyer readiness, payment arrangements, inspection requests, and the practical terms of the proposed transaction before agreeing.

  • Compare offers against your target price.
  • Ask clear questions about payment timing.
  • Agree on price and conditions in writing.
Manage Buyer Enquiries →
05
SAFE TRANSACTION

Secure Payment & Transfer Ownership

Verify that payment has actually cleared before handing over the vehicle. Complete the applicable ownership-transfer formalities and retain copies of signed documents and transaction records.

  • Never rely solely on payment screenshots.
  • Complete the required RTO transfer process.
  • Keep proof of payment and handover.

Have questions about selling your car?

We have answered the most common questions to help you move from listing your vehicle to completing your sale.

Need more help? ↗
Listing fees depend on the options offered by CARVIBE. Review the displayed listing terms before publishing. Optional promotion, inspection, or premium services may involve additional charges.
Consider your car's make, model, age, kilometres driven, condition, service history, ownership, and similar vehicles currently listed in your market. An online estimate is a guide rather than a guaranteed offer.
If listing edits are enabled, sign in to your seller dashboard and open your vehicle listing. Update details such as price, description, and photographs. Some verified information may require additional review.
Check your seller dashboard or enquiry notifications if those features are available. Respond to genuine enquiries, answer questions honestly, and arrange test drives in a safe public location.
Common documents include the Registration Certificate (RC), valid insurance, applicable PUC certificate, identity details, and prescribed ownership-transfer forms. Loan-related hypothecation may also need to be cleared. Confirm the applicable requirements with your local RTO or the official Parivahan portal.
Never share OTPs or banking credentials, pay fees to receive money, or rely only on payment screenshots. Verify that funds have reached your account before handing over the vehicle. Document the transaction and complete the official ownership-transfer process.
