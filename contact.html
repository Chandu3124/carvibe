QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

05 QUESTIONS
01 How do I contact a car seller?

Open the vehicle listing you're interested in and look for the available seller contact options. Review the vehicle details first, then contact the seller to ask about condition, price, ownership documents, and availability. Use only the communication options provided by the marketplace.

02 How can I list my used car on CarVibe?

Sign in to your account and open the vehicle listing section. Enter accurate vehicle information, including make, model, year, mileage, asking price, and condition. Add clear photos and submit the listing according to the platform's requirements.

03 How do I arrange a test drive?

Contact the seller using the details available on the listing and request a suitable date and time. Confirm the meeting location, vehicle availability, and any requirements before travelling. A test drive depends on the seller's availability and agreement.

04 What should I do if I cannot access my account?

Check that you're using the correct login details and try the password recovery option if available. If you still cannot sign in, contact CarVibe support for assistance. Never share your password or verification codes with another person.

05 Does CarVibe verify every listed vehicle?

Verification depends on the checks and services CarVibe actually provides. Review any verification labels and supporting information shown on a listing. Before purchasing, independently check the vehicle's condition, ownership documents, service history, and applicable records.
